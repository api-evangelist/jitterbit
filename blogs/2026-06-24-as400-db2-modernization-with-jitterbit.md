For decades, the IBM AS/400 has delivered unmatched stability and rock-solid security. But in an era of cloud connectivity and artificial intelligence, relying on legacy “green screens” and a shrinking pool of highly paid RPG developers is creating severe operational bottlenecks. Yet, the alternative—a multi-million-dollar “rip and replace” migration to a new ERP—carries catastrophic risk and massive expense.
