Most of us remember college life as a mix of classes, study groups, campus events and late-night cram sessions. But we rarely consider the intricate infrastructure working behind the scenes to make that experience possible, from registration and financial aid to class scheduling, communication tools and digital learning platforms. Modern higher education institutions rely on a sprawling ecosystem of systems: learning management systems (LMS), student information systems (SIS), CRMs, HR platforms and more.
