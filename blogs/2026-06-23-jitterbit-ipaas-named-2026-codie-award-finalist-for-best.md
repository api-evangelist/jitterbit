ALAMEDA, Calif., June 23, 2026 — Jitterbit , the leader in intelligent automation, integration and accountable AI, today announced it has been named a finalist in the 2026 CODiE Awards in the Best Integration Platform category. The CODiE Awards’ Best Integration Platform category honors solutions that excel in connecting diverse environments while simplifying management for all users. Jitterbit iPaaS bridges the gaps between legacy, on-premises and cloud systems to create a modern unified digital ecosystem.
